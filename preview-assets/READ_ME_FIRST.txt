OBDM — site preview
===================

Double-click  index.html  and it opens in your browser. Nothing to install.

  index.html   the main site
  v2/          the bolder alternative concept

Everything is bundled, including the fonts, so it works with no internet.

Two things do need internet, because they stream from elsewhere:
  - the episode audio player (audio is hosted by Libsyn)
  - the outbound links (shop, Discord, Rumble, Twitch, YouTube)

Everything else — layout, fonts, the hero video, all images — is in this folder.

If your browser blocks anything when opening the file directly, double-click
"Open OBDM Site.command" instead. That serves the folder locally and opens it
at http://localhost:8777.
